# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions, accepting responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, harassment, publishing private information, or other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action. Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to gaitan@gmail.com.
